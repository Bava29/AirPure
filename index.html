HOW AIR PURIFIERS WORK

Clean air through a simple purification process.

Air purifiers continuously move indoor air through different purification stages to help capture particles, reduce unwanted odours and improve the overall indoor air environment.

Cleaner Air, Step by Step
01

Draw In Air

Indoor air is pulled through into the purifier through its air intake system.

02

Capture Particles

Filtration stages help capture dust and fine airborne particles from the incoming air.

03

Reduce Odours

Activated carbon filtration can help reduce unwanted household odours from indoor air.

04

Release Clean Air

The purified air is circulated back into the room, for a fresh indoor environment.
